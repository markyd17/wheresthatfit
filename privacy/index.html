Privacy Policy

Where's That Fit? · Effective October 10, 2026

Where's That Fit? ("the app", "we", "us") helps you plan where gear goes in your vehicle and rates the setup. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't sell or share your information for advertising, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

The app is currently in a free beta. If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password. Your password is handled by our login provider and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. We never see it.
  • What you enter in the app: your rig setup (vehicle make, model and year, and your cap and storage configuration), the gear you add (catalog items or your own custom items, with names, weights, sizes and the priority and weather ratings you choose), where you place each item, and the ratings the app calculates for your setup.
  • Technical information: when the app talks to our server, the server records the request (for example, which feature was used and when) and your device's IP address. Our login provider similarly records sign-in activity (such as when you signed in, and from which IP address). We use this to keep the service running, spot problems and block abuse. These logs never contain your password or sign-in tokens.
  • Messages you send us: if you email us or use "Send feedback" in the app, we receive your email address, your message, and the app version and device type the app adds to help us fix problems.

We don't collect your location, contacts, microphone input, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Photos: the current version of the app doesn't access your camera or photos. A future version will let you take or choose a photo of your setup to bring your gear into the app. Before that feature arrives, we'll update this policy to explain how photos are used, stored and deleted, and tell you about the change as described above.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in, and send account emails (confirming your email address and resetting your password).
  • To save your rig and gear and calculate your setup rating.
  • To keep the service secure and working: limiting repeated requests, investigating errors and preventing misuse.
  • To read and reply to your feedback and improve the app.

We don't use your information to build advertising profiles, and we don't sell it.

Who helps us handle it

We use a small number of service providers. They handle your information only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase stores the app's database and runs sign-in and accounts. Data is stored in the United States (US East) and encrypted at rest.
  • Google (Gmail) sends account emails such as sign-up confirmations and password-reset codes, and receives email you send us.
  • Tailscale provides the secure public web address the app connects to. Traffic is encrypted all the way to our server, so Tailscale doesn't see its contents.
  • Apple: if you test the app through TestFlight, Apple collects information under its own privacy policy (for example, crash reports and feedback you choose to send through TestFlight) and shares some of it with us.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety, rights or property of our users, the public or us.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, rig, gear, placements and ratings are kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account (More → Delete account), we immediately delete your account and everything in it. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short time until those backups are replaced.
  • Server logs are kept in a fixed amount of space and overwritten automatically, oldest first, as new entries arrive. Sign-in activity records are kept by our login provider under its own retention limits.
  • Feedback emails are kept as long as they are useful for improving the app.

How we protect it

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). Our server only ever returns your own data to your account, and our database connections are encrypted and verified. No system is perfectly secure, but if we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you promptly.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your data: everything you've entered is visible and editable in the app.
  • Delete your data: delete your account at any time from More → Delete account. If you can't sign in, email us and we'll delete it for you.
  • Get a copy, or ask a question: email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example, California and other US states with privacy laws), you may have additional rights to know what personal information we hold, to correct it, to delete it, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We honor these requests for everyone, wherever they live. We don't sell or "share" personal information as those laws define it.

Children

The app isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Contact us

Questions, requests or concerns: wheresthatfit@gmail.com. Where's That Fit? is operated from the United States.
